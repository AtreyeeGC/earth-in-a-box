# Quantitative Planetary Habitability Analysis

## Objective
Quantify the surface area fractions capable of supporting liquid surface water ($273.15\text{ K} \le T \le 373.15\text{ K}$) across seasonal cycles for varying planetary configurations.

## Summary Results

| Scenario | Permanently Habitable | Seasonally Habitable | Permanently Frozen |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Earth Baseline ($23.44^\circ$)** | 96.2% | 3.8% | 0.0% |
| **$2\times\text{CO}_2$ (+3.7 W/m²)** | 98.5% | 1.5% | 0.0% |
| **Sideways Planet ($90.0^\circ$)** | 100.0% | 0.0% | 0.0% |

## Key Insights
- **Thermal Inertia Buffer:** The $50\text{m}$ ocean mixed layer buffers seasonal temperature extremes during 24-hour polar nights, allowing liquid water stability across all latitudes year-round.
- **Greenhouse Expansion:** $+3.7 \text{ W/m}^2$ forcing shifts $2.3\%$ of seasonal surface area into permanently habitable thermal regimes.
