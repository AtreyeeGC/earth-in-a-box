# 1D Polar Amplification Experiment

## Objective
Demonstrate non-uniform latitudinal warming in response to uniform greenhouse radiative forcing ($+3.7 \text{ W/m}^2$, corresponding to $2\times\text{CO}_2$).

## Physical Mechanisms
1. **Planck Response Damping:** Outgoing longwave flux scales with $4 \epsilon \sigma T^3$. Because polar surface temperatures are much lower than equatorial temperatures, polar regions require a larger temperature increase $\Delta T$ to radiate away an equivalent flux perturbation.
2. **High-Latitude Ice Melt:** Setting the thermal ice threshold to $273.15 \text{ K}$ restricts ice-albedo feedback exclusively to high latitudes.

## Experimental Results
- **Equatorial Warming (0°N):** +2.83 K
- **Polar Warming (90°N):** +3.46 K
- **Polar Amplification Ratio:** 1.22x

## Conclusion
Under uniform greenhouse forcing, polar regions warm 22% faster than equatorial regions, replicating observed planetary climate feedback behavior.
