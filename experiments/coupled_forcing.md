# Coupled CO₂ and Ice-Albedo Feedback Experiment

## Objective
Measure how much dynamic ice-albedo feedback amplifies warming during an atmospheric CO₂ doubling event when ice is present.

## Setup
- **Baseline CO₂:** 280 ppm (Years 0–50)
- **Doubled CO₂:** 560 ppm (Years 50–150)
- **Pre-forcing Temperature (Year 50):** 277.59 K
- **Pre-forcing Albedo (Year 50):** 0.40

## Results
- **Final Temperature (Year 150):** 281.61 K
- **Final Albedo (Year 150):** 0.37
- **Uncoupled Response (Pure Radiative):** +1.48 K
- **Coupled Response (Radiative + Feedback):** +4.02 K
- **Amplification Factor:** ~2.7x

## Scientific Conclusion
When initial planetary conditions allow ice cover to persist at pre-industrial equilibrium, CO₂ doubling initiates a positive feedback cascade: initial radiative warming melts ice, lowering surface albedo from 0.40 to 0.37 and increasing absorbed solar flux. This feedback amplifies total climate warming from +1.48 K to +4.02 K.
